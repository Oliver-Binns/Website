# Accessibility

I want everyone to be able to use this website. It aims to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) at level AAA.

## What this website does

- Every page has landmarks, a "Skip to main content" link and a logical heading structure.
- Text has a contrast ratio of at least 7:1 against its background, in both light and dark mode, including code samples.
- Everything works with a keyboard, with a clear focus indicator. Code samples can be scrolled with the arrow keys.
- Buttons and links outside paragraphs are at least 44 by 44 pixels.
- You can zoom to 400% and content reflows without scrolling sideways.
- Links that open in a new tab say so.
- Images have text alternatives. Nothing moves or plays automatically.

## Known limitations

Some parts of the website don't meet level AAA yet:

- **Unusual words and abbreviations (WCAG 3.1.3 and 3.1.4):** posts use technical terms and abbreviations, such as API, CI and SDK, without definitions or expansions.
- **Reading level (WCAG 3.1.5):** posts are written for software developers and don't have simpler versions. Each post has a short description on the [Writing](/posts/) page.
- **Video (WCAG 1.2.6, 1.2.7 and 1.2.8):** embedded and linked talk videos don't have sign language interpretation, extended audio description or a full transcript.
- **Images of text (WCAG 1.4.9):** some posts include screenshots of apps and developer tools. These show the interface being described, and their alternative text describes what they show.
